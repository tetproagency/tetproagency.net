How we work

We treat a digital brand as one system. Strategy, content, design, engineering and media are scoped together, so decisions in one area do not undo work in another.

Every project starts from your business goals and ends with something you can measure, maintain and extend.

“Xây dựng thương hiệu số” (Vietnamese brand slogan: “Building the digital brand”)

Services

  • SEO

    Technical audits, content structure and on-page optimization so search engines and people can find and understand your site.

  • Web Development

    Fast, accessible, responsive websites built to be easy to update and to grow with your business.

  • Brand Strategy

    Positioning, messaging and visual direction that give your brand a clear and consistent voice.

  • Digital Advertising

    Campaign planning, setup and reporting across paid channels, tied to goals you define.

  • Software Development

    Custom web applications and internal tools, from scoping through release and maintenance.

Project process

  1. Discover

    We review your goals, audience, market and existing assets.

  2. Plan

    We agree on scope, deliverables, timeline and how success is measured.

  3. Build

    We design, develop and produce in stages, with review points along the way.

  4. Launch

    We test, publish and hand over documentation.

  5. Improve

    We review results and prioritize the next round of changes.

Working with international clients

Remote collaboration works best with a clear brief. Here is how to start.

1. Send your brief

Email your goals, website or product link, target markets, timeline and budget range.

2. Define the scope

We reply with questions and a proposed scope: what is included, what is not, and what we need from you.

3. Agree how we communicate

We confirm the contact person, review rhythm and the channels used for updates and approvals.

Frequently asked questions

What types of projects do you take on?

Websites, SEO programs, brand strategy, advertising campaigns and custom software. Tell us your goal and we will say honestly whether we are a fit.

How do we begin?

Send a short brief by email. We will reply with questions and a proposed scope before any work starts.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope and deliverables. We provide a written proposal after reviewing your brief.

Can you work on an existing website or product?

Yes, after we review the current state. An audit is usually the first step.

Who owns the work?

Ownership and licensing terms are set out in the written agreement for each project.

Tell us what you want to build

Share a few lines about your goals and we will respond with next steps.
